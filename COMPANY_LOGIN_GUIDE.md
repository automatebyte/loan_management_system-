# KreditAI Company Admin Login Guide

## 🏢 **FOR APPROVED COMPANIES**

### **Step 1: Get Your Credentials**
After super admin approval, you'll receive an email with:
- **Username**: Your email address (used during registration)
- **Password**: `KreditAI{company_id}!` (e.g., `KreditAI123!`)

### **Step 2: Login Process**
1. **Go to**: https://kreditai1.onrender.com/
2. **Enter**:
   - Username: `your-email@company.com`
   - Password: `KreditAI123!` (replace 123 with your company ID)
3. **Click Login**

### **Step 3: After Login**
- You'll be redirected to Company Admin Dashboard
- Set up your loan officers
- Configure loan products
- Start processing loans

## 🔧 **TROUBLESHOOTING**

### **If Login Fails:**
1. **Check email**: Ensure you're using the exact email from registration
2. **Check password**: Format is `KreditAI{ID}!` where ID is your company number
3. **Contact support**: If still failing, contact super admin

### **Password Reset:**
- Currently manual process
- Contact super admin to reset your password
